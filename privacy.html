SA Government Tender Bid Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy describes the data handling of the SA Government Tender Bid Planner website, published by Clockwork Investment Pty (Ltd). It is an independent static website and does not provide user accounts or a server-side tender workspace. This policy is a plain-language disclosure, not legal advice.

Information entered in the planner

The calculator saves project settings, entered prices, checklist progress and tender text in local storage in the browser on your device. The website's code does not send this planner data or the contents of uploaded PDFs to a publisher-operated backend. The document assistant extracts PDF text and performs optional OCR in your browser. The extracted text can be included in the locally saved project and in a project file you choose to download.

Anyone with access to your browser profile or an exported project file may be able to see its contents. Do not enter passwords, banking credentials or information that you do not have authority to process. Protect exported files as confidential business records when appropriate.

To remove saved planner progress, use the browser's site-data controls for this website. Downloaded project files must be deleted separately using your device's file manager. Clearing site data may also remove the offline app cache.

Third-party services and network requests

The main planner loads Google AdSense advertising code, a Google Tag Manager container and an advertising script from quge5.com. These providers and their advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize or measure ads, including based on prior visits to this or other websites. They may process technical information such as browser and device details, network identifiers and ad interactions. The publisher has not verified which additional tags, if any, are configured inside the Tag Manager container. Each provider's processing is subject to its own terms, settings and privacy disclosures.

The site also loads Tailwind CSS from its public CDN. When you use PDF reading or OCR, the browser downloads the PDF.js and Tesseract libraries from jsDelivr; OCR may also download English language data. These requests go to those providers, but the tender document itself is processed locally by the planner and is not uploaded by its document-processing code. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies in your browser and review Google's advertising technology information, Privacy Policy and ad settings. Browser controls may affect site functionality or advertising personalization.

Public feedback channel

If you report a problem through the project's GitHub Issues page, the information you post is public and handled by GitHub under GitHub's privacy terms. Do not post personal data, confidential tender materials or credentials there. Read the Contact page before using that channel.

Children, changes and questions

This business planning tool is intended for suppliers and business users, not children. We may update this policy when the website or its services change; the date above indicates the latest editorial update. For general questions, use the public feedback route on the Contact page without including personal or confidential information.

This policy describes the website's current design and known integrations. Google's and other third-party services' processing is governed by their own notices. The Tag Manager container can be configured independently of this website's source code.
