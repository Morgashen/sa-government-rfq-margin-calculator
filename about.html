SA Government Tender Bid Planner

About this website

An independent planning resource for South African suppliers preparing quotations and tender responses.

Publisher

SA Government Tender Bid Planner is published by Clockwork Investment Pty (Ltd). It is an independent business planning resource and is not operated, endorsed or approved by the South African Government, National Treasury, SARS or any procuring institution.

What the planner does

The website provides a browser-based RFQ pricing calculator, estimated VAT and tax planning, a preference-points simulator, a bid-readiness checklist and a practical guide to preparing and submitting tender responses. Its tender text helper uses local keyword-based analysis to highlight details for the user to verify; it is not legal advice or a substitute for reading the complete tender pack.

How to use the information

Use calculations and guidance as starting points for your own review. Each procurement has its own instructions and may be affected by changing laws, tax rules, forms and addenda. Confirm requirements with the buyer and relevant official sources before submitting a bid or making a business decision. The planner does not promise that a bid will be compliant, successful or profitable.

Feedback and corrections

We welcome reports of errors and suggestions to improve the tool. See the Contact page for the public feedback channel. Do not send confidential tender documents, personal information or credentials through a public issue tracker.
